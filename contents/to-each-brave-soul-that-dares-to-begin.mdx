2025 started with huge goals. The primary focus was to build something that could make a significant impact. The journey has been filled with ups and downs, moments of clarity, and times of doubt.

However, the market had other plans, and the initial enthusiasm was met with harsh realities. By December, the results looked bleak. It was a year of tough lessons rather than easy victories.

A sincere "Thank You" goes out to the version of us that wasn't afraid to fail. It is easy to stay comfortable, but it takes a brave soul to step into the unknown.

To everyone who started something in 2025 that didn't go as planned:

> The win is in the start.

Daring to think big and daring to act is a profit no market can take away.
